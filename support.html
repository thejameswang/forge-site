Support

How can we help?

The fastest way to reach us is from inside the app.

Contact us: open Forge and go to Settings → Send Feedback in the app. Include what you were doing and what you expected. Beta testers can also use TestFlight → Forge → Send Beta Feedback.

Account

Sign in or create an account: Settings → Account. You can use Apple, Google, or email.

Change who sees your workouts: Settings → Privacy.

Export your data: Settings → Account → Export My Data (social data as JSON). Workouts: Settings → Data → Export.

Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account. You choose whether to keep your workouts on your iPhone.

Training

Weights feel wrong: edit any weight in the workout; the plan learns from what you log. To start over, Plan → ⋯ → Adjust Plan.

Something hurts: Plan → ⋯ → Something Hurts swaps exercises around that area for a few weeks. See a professional if pain persists.

Apple Watch not syncing: open Forge on both devices with Bluetooth on; the watch keeps your workout and sends it when connected.

Safety and reporting

Report a profile, post, comment, or club from its ⋯ menu, and block anyone you don't want to hear from. Read the Community Guidelines.
