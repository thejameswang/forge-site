Plans

A program built for you, adjusted every session.

Answer a few questions about your goal, days, time, equipment, and any sore spots. Forge builds a multi-week program and explains why.

  • Coached sets. Today's weights come from what you actually lifted, with a target effort.
  • Real life friendly. Move a session, shorten it to 30 minutes, or swap an exercise when the rack is taken.
  • Built-in deloads and re-tests so you keep progressing for months.
  • Or pick a program: beginner full body, upper/lower, meet prep, home dumbbells, and more.

FAQ

Questions

How much does Forge cost?

Forge is free, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Logging, plans, the watch app, and progress all work without one. An account adds sharing, friends, clubs, and leaderboards.

Does it work without my phone?

Yes. The Apple Watch app logs a full workout on its own and syncs when your phone is back in range.

Can I bring my history from Strong?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong and import it in Settings → Data.

Is it safe to follow the plan?

Forge adjusts weights from what you log and backs off when sets get missed, but it isn't medical advice. Train with good form, use safety equipment, and talk to a professional about pain or health conditions.

How do I get help?

In the app, go to Settings → Send Feedback in the app. More on the support page.
